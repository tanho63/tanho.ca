## new hobby: dry aging fridge

My latest obsession/hobby has been starting my own meat dry-aging setup! More
notes [here](https://github.com/tanho63/dry-age-monitor), but thus far it's been
fun to tinker with the Raspberry Pi I have monitoring the fridge temperature and
humidity, setting up automated hourly reporting etc. For this first run, I've got
14.2 pounds of ungraded ribeye from Costco Business Centre and aiming for about
45 days (having already had a couple of 30-day aged steaks in the past). Ideally
also trying to keep it around 38F average temperature and 80-85% relative humidity.
I think it'll be fun learnings and eatings, and I did make sure to save a steak
from the same roast to compare un-aged vs dry-aged! I should have the steaks ready
to try by August 27th, at which point I'll probably try to get in a larger bone-in
subprimal from the butcher shop and decide whether to go for 60 days or just
another 45 days, which makes for good timing to serve for late Oct / early Nov
birthdays and the Dec holidays.
